A journey of one's own: up to eight scenes from any journey, in any order, each staying for its own time or skipped. It keeps the sound of the journey it starts from. It goes into a user slot on the FM-1, or plays there straight away, or stays here as a file.

The FM-1 is reached over USB-MIDI, in Chrome or Edge (Web MIDI), with Penumbra 1.13 or later on it. Without it, the page still makes journeys from the presets and from files. Nothing leaves this computer.
