The looper: VOX, kick and BLOOM, on the clock

RECrecord · again: close the loop
REC while it playsoverdub a layer · again: keep it
PLAYstop the loop, or play it from its start
PLAY held, let goundo the last layer
PLAY held + ALGO½ speed · as played · 2×
REC + PLAYclear the loop

Up to 16 bars, 8 layers. It follows the scene's key, and is saved with the journey.

Moving through it

  • SEQ starts and stops the clock; the drone swells in and fades out with it.
  • PRESETS: the next scene. Drone notes move one voice at a time.
  • SELECT on HOME: MORPH by hand; halfway, the key changes.
  • ARP: FREEZE what you hold; again to let it ring out.
  • Black 6 the kick, black 7 BLOOM, 8–11 GLASS · REED · HALO · LOW.

Good to know

  • It saves itself once the sound rests; SAVE saves now.
  • A dot in the header: not saved yet.
  • SLEEP (SYSTEM): everything lets go over 20–90 min.
  • VOX plays from MIDI too (channel 1), with pitch bend.
  • Leave space: the silence is part of it.
