# Penumbra 1.3

A space-drone instrument for the M-VAVE FM-1, by Lunar Eclipse Forge. 1.3 makes HOME's screen show what you
hear.

**Package:** `penumbra-1.3.fwsc`, identity `FM-1_73010300`. It carries the update loader: you can always
leave Penumbra again, for another firmware or M-VAVE's own. **Coming from 1.2.1, 1.2, 1.1 or 1.0:** your
journey, its loop, your user slots, your sounds and your settings come over, as they were.

## New on HOME

- **A journey you can see.** While a scene travels to the next (or you MORPH there), the moon slides off the
  sun and back: the corona fades, a diamond ring flashes, a crescent grows, and the sun shows most at the
  middle, where the key changes. To the next scene the moon goes left; to the one before, right.
- **The drones move as they sound.** Each drone voice's bead is larger, out in the corona, swelling as loud as
  the voice is. The beads slide round the moon toward the side DRONE is panned to, swaying with it, and the
  tide lifts them out and lets them back.
- **Stars in the stereo field.** A note's star comes out on VOX's side and drifts with its sway. If VOX goes
  to the delay, each echo lights the star again, fainter, in time; with PING-PONG, on alternate sides.
- **The looper is a satellite** circling the corona once a loop: a red trail while it records, a bead for each
  layer while it plays.
- **The mark** sits smaller, all of it inside the moon.

The sound is as 1.2.1 left it. 1.2.1's fix for newer FM-1s (the UC25HQ80 flash) is in.
