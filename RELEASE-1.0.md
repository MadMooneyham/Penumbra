# Penumbra 1.0

A space-drone instrument for the M-VAVE FM-1, by Lunar Eclipse Forge. Switch it on and the drone swells in;
you play over it, loop it, shape it and move it from place to place.

**Package:** `penumbra-1.0.fwsc`, identity `FM-1_73010000`. It carries the update loader: you can always
leave Penumbra again, for another firmware or M-VAVE's own.

## What is in it

- **DRONE**: five voices on the black keys, held on the scene's pedal. A new scene moves only the notes it
  must, one voice at a time, sliding slowly. Each voice HELD or SWELL (it comes and goes).
- **VOX**: the white keys, a four-voice synth far back in the shimmer. Four sounds on black keys 8-11
  (GLASS, REED, HALO, LOW): wave from saw to a narrow pulse, low-pass to band-pass, glide.
- **PULSE**: a deep kick tuned to the key (black 6) and BLOOM, a slow swell in the key (black 7).
- **TEXTURE**: a bed of space wind.
- **The looper**: REC records VOX and PULSE on the clock, in layers; PLAY stops or plays (held: undo, with
  ALGO: half or double speed). It follows the scene's key and is saved with the journey.
- **Holds**: hold a drone key, a VOX sound key, BLOOM's key, FX or a white key and the knobs play that:
  a voice's sound, throws (shimmer, feedback, freeze, dip), bends and pitch. SAVE in a hold keeps it.
- **FREEZE** (ARP): the notes held keep sounding. **MORPH** (SELECT on HOME): walk to the next scene by hand.
- **SHAPE** (ENV): four knobs a page for every voice; SEL twice resets a page, SYSTEM > RESET SOUND all of it.
- **Journeys**: six presets of scenes and eight user slots; the one playing, and its loop, survive a power cut.
- **SLEEP**: in 20 to 90 minutes it lets everything go, gently.
- MIDI clock in and out, VOX on a MIDI channel (pitch bend too), USB audio, a screen reader.

The whole map of the controls is in the README.

## From the betas

Journeys, user slots and settings from 0.13 to 0.17 are carried over. The betas' identity was X0X's scheme
(FM-1_9...); from 1.0 Penumbra has its own (FM-1_73...).
